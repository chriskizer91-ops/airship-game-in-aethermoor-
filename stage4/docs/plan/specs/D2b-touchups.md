# Package D2b-touchups: touch-ups after stage 4b's gate

Stage 4b's gate (October 12) found these. Fix each and look at before/after screenshots at laptop (1280x800),
upright-phone (390x844) and sideways-phone (844x390) sizes:

1. **The danger fan reads as pink haze from the chase view.** With a raider level and close alongside (the usual
   fight), her red fan is seen nearly edge-on and shows as a pink haze across the horizon; its rings and shape can't be
   made out, so only the red "Broadside!" tag really warns. From ahead or above it reads well. Make it read as a
   clear fan of danger from the chase view too (for example a brighter, crisper rim along its edges and where it
   reaches the Captain's ship, or more contrast close to her), without hiding the raider or costing a phone more.
2. **Chain shot in flight is tiny at fight range.** At 150 to 250 m it shows as small white-violet sparks; its two
   spinning heads and lilac colour only show close up. Make it read as chain shot at fight range (cheaply: e.g. a
   lilac whirling streak sized to the distance), so a player sees what they loaded. Do the same check for crystal
   breakers (pale blue) against round shot.
3. **Edge tags' arrows don't say which way.** The arrows are small triangles with nearly equal sides, so a tag for a
   raider behind-left looks as if it points up. Make the arrow plainly point toward its raider (a longer, slimmer
   pointer or chevron), at every edge and on a phone.
4. **Your own gunsmoke hides the payoff.** When the Captain fires a broadside, her own smoke often hides the target
   from the chase view for a few seconds, especially on a phone, so a breaker volley's hits can't be seen. Keep the
   smoke, but keep the line of sight to the target she's locked on clearer (for example her own smoke thinning or
   drifting aside near that line, or blown back faster), without changing how raiders' smoke looks.
